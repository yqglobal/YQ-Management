# 1. Purpose

Qmova is committed to continuously improving the privacy, security, and operational maturity of its platform.

This Compliance Roadmap provides transparency into:

- our current compliance posture;
- security capabilities already implemented;
- initiatives that are planned;
- certifications that are under consideration.

This document is intended to help Customers understand where Qmova is today and where we intend to invest in future improvements.

# 7. Certifications

## Current Position

Qmova is currently building its security and compliance program.

At this time we do **not** claim certification for:

- SOC 2
- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27701
- HIPAA
- PCI DSS

We believe transparency is preferable to overstating compliance maturity.

---

## Future Certifications

As Qmova grows, we may evaluate certifications based on customer demand and operational readiness.

Potential future initiatives include:

- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO/IEC 27001
- ISO/IEC 27701

These initiatives are aspirational and should not be interpreted as commitments or timelines.
