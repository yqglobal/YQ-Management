# 1. At a Glance

This Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") governs how Qmova processes Personal Information on behalf of a Customer where the Customer acts as the relevant Data Controller or equivalent Responsible Party.

In plain English:

* **The Customer determines why Customer Data is processed.**
* **Qmova processes Customer Data to provide the Qmova Services.**
* **Qmova will not use Customer Data for unrelated purposes except where permitted by this DPA, the Customer Agreement, or applicable law.**
* **Qmova uses security measures designed to protect Customer Data.**
* **Qmova may use approved Subprocessors to provide parts of the Services.**
* **Qmova will provide reasonable assistance with applicable privacy requests, security incidents, and compliance obligations.**
* **Customers remain responsible for their own legal basis, notices, consents, and processing decisions.**
* **Customer Data can be exported where supported by the Services and applicable agreement.**
* **Customer Data is subject to deletion and retention procedures following termination, subject to legal, security, and backup requirements.**
* **International transfers are handled using safeguards required by applicable law.**

This DPA is intended to establish the contractual processing framework. It does not replace the Customer's responsibility to comply with privacy laws applicable to its own processing.

---

# 2. Purpose and Scope

This Data Processing Addendum forms part of the agreement between Qmova and the Customer under which Qmova provides the Services.

It applies where Qmova processes Personal Information on behalf of the Customer.

This DPA is intended to establish the respective obligations of Qmova and the Customer regarding that processing.

For Customers subject to GDPR, this DPA is intended to address the processor-contract requirements of Article 28 of the GDPR.

For Customers subject to South African privacy law, this DPA is intended to support the contractual and security relationship between a Responsible Party and an Operator under POPIA.

Under GDPR, processor processing must be governed by a binding contract setting out matters including the subject matter and duration of processing, nature and purpose, types of Personal Information, categories of Data Subjects, and the parties' rights and obligations.

Under POPIA, an Operator processes Personal Information for a Responsible Party under a contract or mandate and must process it with the knowledge or authorization of the Responsible Party and maintain appropriate confidentiality and security safeguards.

# 4. Definitions

Capitalized terms used in this DPA have the meanings below.

## Customer

The **Customer** is the organization that subscribes to or otherwise uses Qmova under the Customer Agreement.

## Customer Data

**Customer Data** means information submitted to Qmova by or on behalf of the Customer for processing through the Services.

## Data Controller

A **Data Controller** is the entity that determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Information.

## Data Processor

A **Data Processor** is the entity that processes Personal Information on behalf of a Data Controller.

## Responsible Party

Under POPIA terminology, a **Responsible Party** is the party that determines the purpose of and means for processing Personal Information.

## Operator

Under POPIA terminology, an **Operator** is a person or organization that processes Personal Information for a Responsible Party in terms of a contract or mandate without coming under the direct authority of that Responsible Party.

## Personal Information

**Personal Information** means information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person or equivalent information protected by applicable privacy legislation.

## Data Subject

A **Data Subject** is an identified or identifiable individual whose Personal Information is processed.

## Subprocessor

A **Subprocessor** is a third party appointed by Qmova to process Customer Data on Qmova's behalf in connection with providing the Services.

## Services

The **Services** are the Qmova products and features made available to the Customer under the Customer Agreement.

## Workspace

A **Workspace** is the Customer's logical operating environment within Qmova.

Qmova generally acts as the Customer's Data Processor or Operator when processing Customer Data to provide the Services.

* provide the Platform;
* maintain the Platform;
* provide Customer support;
* secure the Platform;
* prevent abuse;
* perform requested integrations;
* comply with applicable law;

* as permitted by the Customer Agreement;
