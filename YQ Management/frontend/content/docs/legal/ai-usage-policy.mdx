# 1. At a Glance

Qmova may incorporate Artificial Intelligence ("AI") features to improve productivity and operational efficiency.

Our principles are straightforward:

- AI is intended to assist—not replace—human decision-making.
- Customers remain responsible for decisions made using AI-generated outputs.
- We strive to provide transparency about AI-enabled functionality.
- AI features will be designed with privacy and security considerations in mind.
- Customer Data is not used to train third-party AI models unless explicitly stated and agreed.

---

# 2. Purpose

This AI Usage Policy explains how Qmova approaches the design, use, and governance of AI-enabled functionality within the platform.

It should be read together with the:

- Privacy Policy
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA)
- Security Policy
- Terms of Service

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to AI-enabled capabilities provided directly by Qmova.

It does not apply to:

- Customer-developed AI integrations;
- third-party AI tools connected by Customers;
- external services used independently of Qmova.

---

# 4. Our Approach to AI

AI is intended to improve operational workflows such as:

- appointment management;
- Visit administration;
- customer communications;
- reporting;
- analytics;
- operational assistance.

AI is designed to support users, not replace professional judgment.

Customers remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated suggestions before relying on them.

---

# 5. AI Features

AI capabilities may include:

- drafting operational communications;
- summarizing Visit information;
- generating reports;
- workflow recommendations;
- productivity assistance;
- natural language search;
- other AI-assisted functionality introduced over time.

The availability of AI features may vary by subscription plan, region, or product release.

---

# 6. Customer Control

Customers decide whether to use AI-enabled features where they are offered.

Where configurable, Customers may enable or disable AI functionality within their Workspace.

Some core platform functionality may not rely on AI at all.

---

# 7. Customer Data and AI

Customer Data belongs to the Customer.

Qmova does not intentionally use Customer Data to train third-party general-purpose AI models unless:

- the Customer has expressly agreed;
- applicable law permits such processing; and
- the applicable documentation clearly explains the processing.

Where AI providers process Customer Data on Qmova's behalf, they do so in accordance with applicable contractual and privacy obligations.

---

# 8. Human Oversight

AI-generated content should be reviewed by an appropriately authorized user before it is relied upon.

Customers should not rely solely on AI outputs for decisions involving:

- legal obligations;
- regulatory compliance;
- healthcare decisions;
- financial decisions;
- employment decisions;
- public safety.

Human review remains an important part of responsible AI use.

---

# 9. Accuracy and Limitations

AI-generated outputs may be:

- incomplete;
- inaccurate;
- outdated;
- inappropriate for a particular context.

Customers should independently verify information before acting on it.

Qmova does not guarantee that AI-generated outputs are correct, complete, or suitable for every purpose.

---

# 10. Responsible AI Principles

Qmova seeks to design AI functionality consistent with the following principles:

- transparency;
- accountability;
- privacy;
- security;
- fairness;
- reliability;
- human oversight.

These principles guide product development but do not constitute a guarantee that AI outputs will always meet every expectation or use case.

---

# 11. Third-Party AI Services

Some AI functionality may rely on third-party providers.

Where applicable, such providers may process information solely to provide the requested AI functionality.

Qmova evaluates providers based on factors including:

- security;
- reliability;
- contractual protections;
- privacy commitments.

Current subprocessors are listed separately in the Subprocessor List.

---

# 12. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- reviewing AI-generated outputs;
- verifying important information;
- complying with applicable laws;
- determining whether AI is appropriate for their workflows;
- ensuring that authorized personnel use AI responsibly.

Customers should avoid submitting information to AI features that they are not authorized to process.

---

# 13. AI Development and Improvement

Qmova may improve AI-enabled functionality over time.

Improvements may include:

- better accuracy;
- improved usability;
- new capabilities;
- enhanced performance;
- additional safeguards.

Changes may occur without altering the fundamental principles described in this policy.

---

# 14. Security and Privacy

AI features are subject to Qmova's broader privacy and security program.

Appropriate safeguards may include:

- authentication;
- authorization;
- encrypted communications;
- audit logging where appropriate;
- access controls;
- monitoring.

Additional information is available in the Privacy Policy and Security Policy.

---

# 15. Future AI Features

As Qmova evolves, additional AI-assisted capabilities may be introduced.

Future features will be documented through product documentation, release notes, or updates to this policy where appropriate.
