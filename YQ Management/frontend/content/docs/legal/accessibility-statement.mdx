# 1. At a Glance

Qmova is committed to building a Visit Management Platform that is usable by as many people as reasonably possible, regardless of ability or the technology they use.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort rather than a one-time project. As Qmova evolves, we aim to continuously improve the accessibility of our applications, documentation, and customer experience.

We welcome feedback that helps us identify and address accessibility barriers.

---

# 2. Purpose

This Accessibility Statement explains Qmova's approach to accessibility and our ongoing efforts to improve the usability of our platform.

It is intended to provide transparency for Customers, Users, and Visitors regarding accessibility practices and future improvements.

---

# 3. Our Commitment

Qmova aims to:

- design interfaces that are clear and consistent;
- support keyboard navigation where practical;
- use semantic HTML where appropriate;
- provide meaningful labels for interactive elements;
- maintain sufficient color contrast where practical;
- improve compatibility with assistive technologies over time;
- consider accessibility throughout product development.

Accessibility is incorporated into product design, engineering, and quality assurance as the platform evolves.

---

# 4. Scope

This statement applies to:

- the Qmova web application;
- the Qmova public website;
- public documentation;
- customer-facing portals;
- administrative interfaces where applicable.

Third-party websites and services linked from Qmova may have their own accessibility practices.

---

# 5. Accessibility Principles

Qmova seeks to design experiences that are:

### Perceivable

Information should be presented in ways that can be perceived by a wide range of users.

### Operable

Core functionality should be usable through a variety of input methods, including keyboard navigation where supported.

### Understandable

Interfaces should use consistent terminology, predictable navigation, and clear messaging.

### Robust

Where practical, Qmova aims to build interfaces that work across modern browsers, devices, and assistive technologies.

These principles are informed by generally accepted accessibility practices.

---

# 6. Accessibility Features

Depending on the feature and interface, Qmova may provide:

- semantic page structure;
- descriptive form labels;
- keyboard-accessible controls;
- visible focus indicators;
- accessible error messaging;
- responsive layouts;
- scalable text through browser zoom;
- consistent navigation;
- accessible tables where appropriate.

Accessibility support may continue to improve as new features are introduced.

---

# 7. Browser and Device Compatibility

Qmova is designed to support current versions of major modern browsers, including:

- Google Chrome
- Mozilla Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Apple Safari

The platform is also designed to function across common desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Performance and accessibility may vary depending on browser configuration, operating system, assistive technology, or unsupported legacy browsers.

---

# 8. Known Limitations

Although accessibility is an ongoing priority, some areas of the platform may not yet provide an optimal experience for every user.

Examples may include:

- newly released features that are still being refined;
- third-party embedded components;
- customer-configured content;
- browser-specific behaviors.

Where accessibility issues are identified, Qmova aims to evaluate and address them as part of normal product development.

---

# 9. Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is part of Qmova's continuous improvement process.

Future enhancements may include:

- expanded keyboard support;
- improved screen reader compatibility;
- additional accessibility testing;
- enhanced color contrast validation;
- improved documentation accessibility;
- accessibility reviews during feature development.

Improvement priorities are guided by customer feedback, technical feasibility, and product roadmap considerations.

---

# 10. Third-Party Content

Certain functionality may rely on third-party services such as:

- authentication providers;
- embedded maps;
- communication services;
- analytics tools;
- customer-configured integrations.

Qmova does not control the accessibility of third-party services but encourages the use of providers that maintain strong accessibility practices.

**privacy@qmova.com**

- the page or feature involved;

- the operating system;
- any assistive technology being used;
