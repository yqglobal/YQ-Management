# 1. At a Glance

This Service Level Agreement explains how Qmova operates and supports the platform.

It describes:

- how we approach platform availability;
- planned and emergency maintenance;
- incident management;
- customer support expectations;
- backup and recovery practices;
- responsibilities shared between Qmova and Customers.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, this SLA is informational and applies to the standard Qmova service.

Enterprise Customers may have separately negotiated service commitments.

---

# 2. Purpose

This SLA establishes Qmova's operational commitments and explains how we deliver and maintain the Services.

It should be read together with:

- Terms of Service
- Security Policy
- Trust Center
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

---

# 3. Scope

This SLA applies to:

- Qmova web application;
- APIs;
- authentication;
- Visit management;
- Appointment scheduling;
- Queue management;
- QR Check-in;
- analytics;
- notifications.

It does not apply to:

- Customer-owned infrastructure;
- Customer internet connectivity;
- third-party software outside Qmova's control;
- Customer-managed integrations.

---

# 4. Definitions

**Availability** means the ability to access the Qmova Services.

**Incident** means an unplanned interruption or degradation of the Services.

**Maintenance** means planned or emergency work performed on the platform.

**Customer** means the organization using Qmova.

**Workspace** means a Customer's tenant within Qmova.

---

# 5. Service Availability

Qmova is designed for reliable operation and continuous improvement.

We implement measures including:

- infrastructure monitoring;
- application health checks;
- logging and alerting;
- redundancy where appropriate;
- backup procedures;
- security monitoring.

Qmova does **not** guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation.

Availability may occasionally be affected by:

- maintenance;
- infrastructure failures;
- internet connectivity;
- third-party provider outages;
- security incidents;
- events outside our reasonable control.

Where a formal uptime commitment is agreed with an Enterprise Customer, it will be documented separately.

---

# 6. Planned Maintenance

From time to time, Qmova performs planned maintenance to:

- deploy improvements;
- apply security updates;
- upgrade infrastructure;
- improve reliability.

Where reasonably practicable, planned maintenance will be scheduled to minimize customer disruption.

---

# 7. Emergency Maintenance

Emergency maintenance may be required to:

- address critical security vulnerabilities;
- restore service;
- protect Customer Data;
- respond to infrastructure failures.

Emergency maintenance may occur without advance notice where necessary.

---

# 8. Support

Customers may contact:

- support@qmova.com
- security@qmova.com (security issues)
- privacy@qmova.com (privacy requests)
- legal@qmova.com (legal matters)

Support is provided on a reasonable-efforts basis unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Response times may vary depending on:

- issue severity;
- complexity;
- available information;
- business hours.

---

# 9. Incident Management

When a significant incident occurs, Qmova aims to:

1. identify the issue;
2. investigate the cause;
3. mitigate customer impact;
4. restore service;
5. verify stability;
6. perform post-incident review where appropriate.

Where appropriate, affected Customers may receive updates regarding major service disruptions.

---

# 10. Customer Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- maintaining accurate account information;
- protecting their credentials;
- assigning appropriate Workspace permissions;
- complying with the Acceptable Use Policy;
- maintaining their own internet connectivity;
- exporting operational data where required;
- configuring Customer-owned integrations.

Customers are also responsible for the information they choose to store in Qmova.

---

# 11. Backups and Disaster Recovery

Qmova maintains backup and recovery procedures designed to support business continuity.

Backups are intended for disaster recovery and operational resilience.

Backup systems are **not** intended to function as a customer archive.

Deletion of Customer Data from production systems does not necessarily result in immediate deletion from backup media.

Further information is available in the Data Retention Policy.

---

# 12. Security

Qmova applies administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Services.

Examples include:

- HTTPS/TLS encryption;
- secure authentication;
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC);
- tenant isolation;
- audit logging;
- infrastructure monitoring;
- password hashing;
- secure session management.

Additional information is available in the Security Policy and Trust Center.

---

# 13. Service Limitations

Qmova's Services depend on third-party infrastructure and internet connectivity.

Accordingly, Qmova cannot guarantee uninterrupted access under all circumstances.

Examples include:

- cloud provider outages;
- ISP failures;
- DNS failures;
- force majeure events;
- widespread internet disruptions.

---

# 14. Third-Party Dependencies

Certain functionality relies on third-party providers, including:

- authentication;
- email delivery;
- cloud infrastructure;
- WhatsApp connectivity.

Service availability may therefore be affected by outages experienced by these providers.

A current list of providers is available in the Subprocessor List.

---

# 15. Communication

Where practical, Qmova will communicate significant operational events through appropriate channels.

Examples include:

- maintenance notices;
- security notifications;
- incident updates;
- policy changes.

Communication methods may include:

- email;
- in-product notifications;
- documentation updates.

---

# 16. SLA Exclusions

This SLA does not apply to interruptions caused by:

- Customer misuse;
- unsupported configurations;
- Customer-developed integrations;
- Customer-owned infrastructure;
- force majeure events;
- actions prohibited by the Acceptable Use Policy.

---

# 17. Changes to this Agreement

Qmova may update this SLA from time to time to reflect:

- operational improvements;
- platform changes;
- infrastructure changes;
- legal requirements;
- support process improvements.

Material updates will be reflected by a new version number and publication date.
