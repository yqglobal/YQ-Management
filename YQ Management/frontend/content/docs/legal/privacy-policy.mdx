# 1. At a Glance

Qmova is a Visit Management Platform that helps organizations manage customer interactions from booking through completion.

In simple terms:

* **We do not sell Personal Information.**
* **Customers control the Customer Data they place in their Qmova Workspace.**
* **Qmova generally processes Customer Data on behalf of the Customer.**
* **Qmova may process certain information for its own purposes**, including account administration, security, billing, support, and operation of the Qmova service.
* **Customers connect their own WhatsApp infrastructure.** Qmova does not own Customer WhatsApp numbers or use a shared Qmova-owned WhatsApp identity for Customer messaging.
* **We use third-party providers** to operate specific parts of the service, such as authentication, email, hosting, and messaging infrastructure.
* **We use security controls** including authentication, authorization, RBAC, tenant isolation, audit logging, session controls, monitoring, and encryption in transit.
* **You may have rights** to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or otherwise control the use of your Personal Information depending on applicable law.
* **Customers remain responsible for their own legal obligations** when using Qmova to collect and process Visitor information.
* **Qmova is not an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system.** Healthcare Customers remain responsible for determining what information they store and for complying with applicable healthcare laws.

This section is intended to make the policy easier to understand. It does not replace the detailed provisions below.

# 5. Qmova's Role in Processing

Qmova may act in different privacy roles depending on the information and purpose involved.

## 5.1 Qmova as Data Controller

Qmova generally acts as a Data Controller when it determines the purposes and means of processing information for its own business and operational purposes.

Examples may include:

* Qmova account registration;
* Qmova website operations;
* security monitoring;
* fraud prevention;
* customer support;
* billing;
* legal compliance;
* platform administration;
* direct communications from Qmova.

## 5.2 Qmova as Data Processor

Qmova generally acts as a Data Processor where a Customer determines why and how Customer Data is processed and Qmova processes that information to provide the Platform.

Examples may include:

* Visitor contact information;
* Appointment information;
* Walk-in records;
* Queue records;
* Visit records;
* Service information;
* Staff information;
* notification information;
* Customer-configured operational notes.

The distinction between controller and processor depends on the actual processing activity and applicable law. Under GDPR principles, the controller determines the purposes and means of processing, while a processor processes Personal Information on behalf of the controller.

Where Qmova acts as a Processor, the Customer remains responsible for the underlying processing decisions and generally for responding to requests from its own data subjects.

# 13. Customer-Owned WhatsApp

Qmova uses a Customer-owned model for supported WhatsApp workflows.

The architecture is:

```text
Customer
   │
   │ Owns and controls
   ▼
Customer WhatsApp Environment
   │
   │ Customer-authorized connection
   ▼
Evolution API
   │
   ▼
Qmova
   │
   ▼
Customer Visit Workflow
```

This means:

* the Customer owns its WhatsApp number;
* the Customer controls its WhatsApp environment;
* the Customer determines which messages are sent;
* Qmova does not own the Customer's WhatsApp number;
* Qmova does not use a shared Qmova-owned WhatsApp identity for Customer messaging;
* Qmova provides infrastructure supporting the Customer's messaging workflows;
* the Customer remains responsible for its messaging practices and applicable WhatsApp requirements.

Qmova does not become the owner of Customer conversations merely because the Customer connects its WhatsApp environment to the Platform.

A Customer may disconnect its supported WhatsApp integration according to the available product functionality.
