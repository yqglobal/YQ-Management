# Refund Policy

Qmova provides infrastructure for digital queuing, appointments, multi-step workflows, and payment facilitation. Because our platform serves both Business Tenants and End-Users, our refund policy is divided into two parts.

## 1. Qmova Platform Subscriptions (Tenant to Qmova)

**Free Trial & Cancellations:**
Qmova offers a 14-day free trial on all new accounts so you can evaluate the platform before making a payment. You may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

**Subscription Refunds:**
Once a subscription is paid for, we do not offer refunds for the current billing cycle, whether monthly or annual. When you cancel your subscription, your account will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, your account will be downgraded to the free tier (if applicable) or suspended.

**Billing Errors:**
If you were charged incorrectly due to a technical error on our part, please contact our support team within 30 days of the charge, and we will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge.

---

## 2. End-User Payments (End-User to Tenant)

Qmova facilitates payments directly between End-Users and Tenants using Stripe Connect. When an End-User books a service or progresses through a multi-step workflow that requires payment, the transaction is processed directly into the Tenant's payment account. 

**Qmova's Role as a Facilitator:**
Qmova acts solely as a technology provider and does not hold the principal funds. Qmova automatically deducts a platform application fee (commission) at the time of the transaction, and the remainder is deposited directly to the Tenant.

**Refunds and Disputes:**
* **No Automated Refunds by Qmova:** Qmova **does not** automatically process refunds for cancelled tickets, skipped workflow steps, or missed appointments. 
* **Tenant Responsibility:** All refund requests, payment disputes, and chargebacks must be directed strictly to the Business Tenant providing the service. Tenants have full discretion and control over issuing refunds from their own connected Stripe accounts based on their own independent business policies.
* **Customer Support:** If an End-User attempts to cancel a paid visit through our automated systems (e.g., via the tracking page or WhatsApp chatbot), the cancellation will be blocked, and the End-User will be instructed to contact the Tenant's support email directly to request cancellation and a refund.

Qmova cannot arbitrate disputes between End-Users and Tenants regarding service quality, wait times, or refund eligibility.

## 3. Contact Us

If you are a Tenant and have questions about this policy or your subscription, please contact our support team at support@qmova.com.

If you are an End-User seeking a refund for a service booked through Qmova, please contact the specific business (Tenant) you booked with.
