# Refund Policy

## 1. Subscriptions

Qmova offers a 14-day free trial on all new accounts so you can evaluate the platform before making a payment. 
Once a subscription is paid for, we do not offer refunds for the current billing cycle, whether monthly or annual. 
You may cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

## 2. Cancellations

When you cancel your subscription, your account will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle. After that, your account will be downgraded to the free tier (if applicable) or suspended.

## 3. Exceptions

If you were charged incorrectly due to a technical error on our part, please contact our support team within 30 days of the charge, and we will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge.

## 4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this refund policy, please contact our support team at support@qmova.com.
