# Data Requests

At Qmova, we take your privacy and data ownership seriously. Under applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, and POPIA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

## 1. Requesting Your Data

You can request an export of all data associated with your workspace directly from the **Privacy & Compliance** section in your dashboard. This automated tool will generate a secure, machine-readable JSON file containing all your customers, staff, queues, and visits.

## 2. Data Deletion

To request the complete deletion of your workspace and all associated data, you can use the **Delete Account** option in the **Privacy & Compliance** settings. Please note that this action is irreversible.

## 3. Contacting the DPO

If you are an end-customer of a business that uses Qmova, you must contact that business directly to request access to or deletion of your data, as they are the Data Controller. We act as a Data Processor and cannot delete data on behalf of a controller without their explicit instruction.

If you have legal or compliance-related inquiries regarding data requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@qmova.com.
