# Qmova Trust Center

**Intended Audience:** Customers, prospective enterprise customers, security teams, privacy teams, procurement teams, and other stakeholders evaluating Qmova

---

## At a Glance

Qmova is a Visit Management Platform that helps organizations manage customer visits from booking through completion.

Our approach to trust is built around a few simple principles:

* **Customers control their operational data.** Organizations using Qmova determine how their Customer Data is collected and used within their Workspace.
* **Qmova operates as infrastructure.** We provide the software and systems used to manage Appointments, Walk-ins, Queues, QR Check-ins, Visits, Notifications, and related workflows.
* **Privacy and security are designed into the platform.** Access controls, authentication, audit logging, session management, policy versioning, and other safeguards are part of the platform architecture.
* **Customer-owned WhatsApp infrastructure remains customer-controlled.** Customers connect their own WhatsApp environment rather than using a Qmova-owned WhatsApp identity for customer messaging.
* **We aim for transparency.** Our legal, privacy, security, compliance, and operational documentation is designed to explain how Qmova operates without overstating our current certifications or capabilities.
* **International use is supported.** Qmova is designed for customers operating across jurisdictions and considers applicable privacy requirements, including South African POPIA and GDPR-compatible privacy principles, where relevant.
* **No unsupported certifications.** Qmova does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or other certifications unless and until those certifications have actually been obtained.

## 2. What Qmova Does

Qmova is a multi-tenant, white-label Visit Management Platform.

The platform is built around the concept of a **Visit**.

A Visit can originate from:

* an Appointment;
* a Walk-in;
* a Queue;
* a Referral;
* a Staff-created Visit.

Customers can use Qmova to manage workflows including:

* Appointment Scheduling;
* Walk-in management;
* Queue management;
* QR Check-in;
* Visit tracking;
* Notifications;
* Staff workflows;
* analytics and reporting.

Qmova is designed to support organizations such as:

* hospitals and clinics;
* government offices;
* universities;
* banks;
* retail organizations;
* restaurants;
* salons;
* service centers;
* visa centers; and
* municipal services.

Qmova is not itself the service provider in these interactions. The Customer remains responsible for its relationship with its Visitors and for the services it provides to them.

## 3. Our Trust Principles

### 3.1 Customer Control

Customers use Qmova to operate their own customer-facing workflows.

The Customer determines:

* what information it collects;
* which Services it provides;
* which Visit information is recorded;
* which Staff Members receive access;
* how its Workspace is configured;
* which integrations it enables; and
* how it uses information generated through its operations.

Qmova provides the infrastructure used to operate these workflows.

---

### 3.2 Data Responsibility

Qmova distinguishes between information that we process for our own operational purposes and information that we process on behalf of Customers.

For Customer Data, Qmova generally operates as a service provider or Data Processor, while the Customer determines the purposes for which that information is processed.

For information relating to Qmova's own accounts, website, support operations, security, billing, and administration, Qmova may act as the relevant Data Controller.

The exact allocation of responsibilities is described in the Qmova Privacy Policy and, where applicable, the Qmova Data Processing Addendum.

---

### 3.3 Least-Privilege Access

Qmova uses role-based access controls to limit what authenticated users can access within the platform.

Customer administrators are responsible for assigning appropriate roles and permissions within their Workspace.

Qmova also maintains administrative and security controls intended to restrict internal access to information to authorized personnel and systems.

---

### 3.4 Accountability

Qmova uses audit and policy-versioning mechanisms to improve accountability.

Depending on the event, the platform can record information such as:

* authentication events;
* policy acceptance;
* policy version;
* account activity;
* configuration changes;
* administrative actions; and
* other security or audit events.

Logs and records are retained according to applicable operational and legal requirements.

## 5. Who Controls the Data?

For Customer Data, the primary responsibilities are divided as follows:

| Party                | Primary responsibility                                                                                       |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Visitor              | Provides information to the Customer where requested                                                         |
| Customer             | Determines why and how Customer Data is collected and used                                                   |
| Qmova                | Provides the platform and processes Customer Data to provide the service                                     |
| Third-party provider | Provides a specific infrastructure or integration service under its own contractual and security obligations |

This distinction is important because Qmova generally does not determine the underlying business purpose for which a Customer collects information from its Visitors.

Customers remain responsible for ensuring that their collection and use of personal information is lawful and appropriate for their business and industry.

## 7. Customer-Owned WhatsApp Infrastructure

Qmova's WhatsApp architecture is designed around Customer ownership.

A Customer connects its own WhatsApp environment to Qmova rather than relying on a Qmova-owned WhatsApp number for customer communication.

The intended relationship is:

```text
Customer
   │
   │ Owns and controls
   ▼
Customer WhatsApp Number
   │
   │ Connected by Customer
   ▼
WhatsApp Integration
   │
   ▼
Qmova Infrastructure
   │
   ▼
Customer's Visit Workflows
```

This means:

* Qmova does not own the Customer's WhatsApp number.
* Qmova does not represent itself as the Customer when communicating through the Customer's connected messaging environment.
* The Customer controls which messaging environment it connects.
* The Customer remains responsible for its messaging practices and communications.
* Qmova provides infrastructure used to support the Customer's workflows.
* Customers can disable or disconnect supported messaging integrations.

Qmova does not maintain a Qmova-owned WhatsApp sender identity for Customer messaging as part of this architecture.

Further details concerning messaging responsibilities and third-party providers are addressed in the Qmova Privacy Policy, DPA, and Subprocessor List.

## 8. Authentication and Account Security

Qmova supports multiple authentication mechanisms, including:

* Google OAuth;
* email authentication; and
* OTP-based authentication.

Authentication is designed to provide users with secure access to their Qmova accounts while minimizing unnecessary information collection.

Qmova also maintains account and session management functionality intended to help users and administrators manage access to their accounts.

Where supported by the platform, users can review active sessions and initiate account deletion through account settings.

Authentication events and other security-relevant activity may be logged for security, operational, and audit purposes.

## 9. Access Control and Auditability

Qmova uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to manage permissions within Customer Workspaces.

The purpose of RBAC is to ensure that users receive access appropriate to their responsibilities rather than automatically receiving unrestricted access.

Qmova also maintains audit functionality for relevant administrative and operational events.

Depending on the feature and configuration, audit records may include:

* who performed an action;
* what type of action occurred;
* when the action occurred;
* the relevant Workspace or resource; and
* information necessary to investigate or understand the event.

Audit logging is intended to support accountability, security investigations, operational troubleshooting, and Customer administration.

## 11. Sensitive and Industry-Specific Information

Qmova serves industries in which Customers may process information subject to additional legal or sector-specific requirements.

For example, a healthcare organization may use Qmova to coordinate Visits, appointments, or service workflows involving patients.

Qmova is a **Visit Management Platform**, not an Electronic Health Record (EHR) system.

Customers determine what information they enter into their Workspace and are responsible for ensuring that their use of Qmova complies with applicable industry-specific requirements.

Customers should not assume that the availability of a field in Qmova means that the collection or processing of that information is legally required or appropriate.

Where a Customer's use case involves sensitive or regulated information, the Customer is responsible for evaluating whether Qmova's configured functionality is appropriate for that use case and for implementing any additional safeguards required by applicable law.

Qmova uses cloud infrastructure and managed services to operate the platform.

## 15. Data Retention and Deletion

Qmova follows a retention approach intended to avoid keeping personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose, contractual requirements, security needs, or legal obligations.

Retention can differ depending on the category of data.

Relevant considerations may include:

* the Customer's subscription;
* Customer configuration;
* legal obligations;
* security and audit requirements;
* dispute resolution;
* backup lifecycle; and
* operational requirements.

Customer Data does not necessarily disappear from every backup system at the same moment that it is deleted from primary production systems.

Where applicable, deletion processes include:

```text
Deletion Request
       │
       ▼
Identity / Authorization Check
       │
       ▼
Primary Data Deletion
       │
       ▼
Relevant Secondary Systems
       │
       ▼
Backup Expiration
       │
       ▼
Deletion Lifecycle Complete
```

Detailed retention rules will be maintained in the Qmova Data Retention Policy.
