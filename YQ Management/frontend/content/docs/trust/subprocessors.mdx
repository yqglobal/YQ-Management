**Version:** 1.0.0

**Last Updated:** 12 August 2026

# At a Glance

Qmova uses a limited number of carefully selected third-party service providers ("Subprocessors") to deliver, secure, and operate the Qmova platform.

We select subprocessors based on their ability to support reliable platform operations while helping us protect Customer Data and maintain appropriate security standards.

We aim to minimize the number of subprocessors used and regularly review our providers as our platform evolves.

This page identifies the primary subprocessors that may process Customer Data on behalf of Qmova.

---

# What is a Subprocessor?

A Subprocessor is a third-party service provider engaged by Qmova to process Customer Data on behalf of our Customers.

Examples include providers used for:

- cloud hosting
- email delivery
- authentication
- infrastructure
- communications
- monitoring

A subprocessor is different from a Customer's own service providers or integrations.

---

# Current Subprocessors

| Provider | Purpose | Customer Data Processed | Data Location |
|-----------|---------|------------------------|---------------|
| Google OAuth | User authentication | Account identity information required for authentication | Global infrastructure |
| Brevo | Transactional email and OTP delivery | Email address, email content, OTP messages | Provider infrastructure |
| Evolution API | Customer-owned WhatsApp connectivity | WhatsApp message metadata and content transmitted on behalf of the Customer | Customer-configured infrastructure |
| PostgreSQL | Primary application database | Customer Data, Visit records, Workspace configuration | Qmova infrastructure |
| Redis | Caching, queues, session support | Temporary operational data and queue state | Qmova infrastructure |
| Hostinger | Cloud hosting and infrastructure | Application hosting and storage infrastructure | Provider infrastructure |
| Cloudflare (if used) | DNS, CDN, security and performance | Limited network and request metadata | Global network |

---

# Customer-Owned WhatsApp

Qmova uses Evolution API to facilitate Customer messaging.

Important distinctions:

- Customers own their WhatsApp Business accounts.
- Customers control their WhatsApp numbers.
- Customers control message recipients.
- Customers control message content.
- Qmova does not operate a shared WhatsApp sender identity.

Evolution API acts as connectivity infrastructure between the Customer's WhatsApp environment and Qmova.

---

# Authentication Providers

Customers may authenticate using:

- Google OAuth
- Email and password
- One-Time Password (OTP)

Authentication providers process only the information necessary to authenticate Users and secure access to the platform.

---

# Infrastructure Providers

Infrastructure providers may process Customer Data solely as required to operate the Qmova platform.

Examples include:

- hosting
- databases
- networking
- storage
- application availability
- disaster recovery

Infrastructure providers are not authorized to use Customer Data for their own independent purposes except where required by law or their contractual obligations.

---

# Email Providers

Qmova uses Brevo for operational email delivery.

Examples include:

- account verification
- password reset
- one-time passwords (OTP)
- transactional notifications
- security notifications

Marketing communications, where applicable, are managed separately from essential transactional emails.

---

# Data Processing Expectations

Qmova expects subprocessors to maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the services they provide.

Selection considerations may include:

- security practices
- privacy commitments
- reliability
- contractual protections
- operational maturity

Use of a provider does not imply certification by Qmova.

---

# International Data Transfers

Some subprocessors operate global infrastructure.

As a result, Customer Data may be processed in multiple jurisdictions depending on:

- Customer location
- infrastructure design
- provider architecture
- disaster recovery
- redundancy

Where required, Qmova implements appropriate contractual or legal safeguards for international transfers.

Additional information is available in the Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

---

# Changes to Subprocessors

Qmova may add, replace, or remove subprocessors as our platform evolves.

Reasons may include:

- improved security
- improved reliability
- feature expansion
- operational efficiency
- business continuity

Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Enterprise Customers with contractual notification rights will be notified in accordance with their agreement where applicable.
